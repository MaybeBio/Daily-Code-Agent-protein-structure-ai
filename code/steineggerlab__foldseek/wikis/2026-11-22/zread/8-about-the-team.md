

The Foldseek project is powered by a diverse team of computational biologists, software engineers, and researchers from Seoul National University and beyond. This international collaboration brings together expertise in bioinformatics, machine learning, and structural biology to create tools that are transforming how scientists analyze protein structures at scale.

## Leadership: Martin Steinegger

At the helm is **Martin Steinegger**, an Associate Professor in the Biology Department at Seoul National University and a visionary in computational biology. Martin's journey in bioinformatics began with studies at Technical University Munich and Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich, followed by a Ph.D. in Computer Science from TU Munich in collaboration with the Max Planck Institute for Biophysical Chemistry. His postdoctoral work at Johns Hopkins University further solidified his expertise in large-scale sequence analysis.

Martin is widely recognized for creating groundbreaking computational tools that have become essential in the field. Before Foldseek, he developed **MMseqs2** (which can run 10,000 times faster than BLAST) and **ColabFold** (which democratized protein structure prediction). His contributions to the field were recognized with the 2024 Overton Award from the International Society for Computational Biology. Notably, he was the only author outside of DeepMind on the landmark 2021 Nature paper introducing AlphaFold2.

Sources: [Martin Steinegger | The Institute](https://www.theinstitute.com/fellow/martin-steinegger), [Steinegger Lab](https://steineggerlab.com/en)

## Core Contributors

### Milot Mirdita: The Engine Behind the Code

**Milot Mirdita** serves as a postdoctoral researcher at Seoul National University and is a driving force behind Foldseek's development. With a Ph.D. in Computer Science (summa cum laude) from the University of Göttingen and MPI-NAT, Milot brings deep technical expertise to the team. His work spans bioinformatics, metagenomics, and protein structure prediction, making him instrumental in implementing Foldseek's innovative algorithms.

Milot is also the developer and maintainer of ColabFold and MMseqs2, showcasing his ability to create tools that balance computational efficiency with biological accuracy. His contributions have been essential in making Foldseek **600,000 times faster** than traditional structural aligners while maintaining comparable sensitivity.

Sources: [Dr. Milot Mirdita](https://mirdita.de), [Steinegger Lab](https://steineggerlab.com/en/authors/milot)

**Charlotte Tumescheit** (schnamo) brings a unique background combining mathematics, software development, and RNA structure prediction. Sources: [Charlotte Tumescheit](https://www.vibconferences.be/speaker/charlotte-tumescheit), [Steinegger Lab](https://steineggerlab.com/en)

### Woosub Kim: Pushing the Boundaries of Protein Complex Analysis

**Woosub Kim** has been instrumental in extending Foldseek's capabilities to protein complexes through his work on **Foldseek-Multimer**. His research, published in Nature Methods in 2025, introduced a method that is 3-4 orders of magnitude faster than existing tools for protein complex alignment while maintaining comparable accuracy.

Woosub's work enables the comparison of billions of complex pairs in just 11 hours, opening new possibilities for understanding protein interactions at an unprecedented scale. His contributions represent the team's commitment to not just improving existing methods but fundamentally reimagining what's possible in structural bioinformatics.

Sources: [Rapid and sensitive protein complex alignment with Foldseek-Multimer](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39910251), [Steinegger Lab](https://steineggerlab.com/en)

## The Team's Impact and Philosophy

The Foldseek team operates under a philosophy of **open science and open-source development**. Their tools have been collectively installed and utilized millions of times by researchers worldwide, democratizing access to cutting-edge computational biology methods.

What makes this team exceptional is their ability to bridge fundamental research with practical software development. They don't just publish papers—they create tools that solve real problems for scientists working with protein structures. Their commitment to performance optimization (achieving speedups of 4-5 orders of magnitude) while maintaining biological accuracy has set new standards in the field.

The team's collaborative spirit extends beyond Seoul National University, with contributions from researchers at institutions like the University of Zurich, Johns Hopkins University, and the Max Planck Institute. This global network ensures that Foldseek benefits from diverse perspectives and expertise.

Sources: [Steinegger Lab Datasets - Registry of Open Data on AWS](https://registry.opendata.aws/steineggerlab), [Fast and accurate protein structure search with Foldseek](https://snu.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/fast-and-accurate-protein-structure-search-with-foldseek)
