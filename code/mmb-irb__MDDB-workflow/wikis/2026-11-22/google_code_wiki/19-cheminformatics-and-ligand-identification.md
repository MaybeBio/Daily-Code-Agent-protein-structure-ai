### Cheminformatics and Ligand Identification
The system provides capabilities for identifying and characterizing non-standard residues and ligands present within molecular systems. This includes generating unique identifiers for chemical compounds and integrating information from various external chemical and lipid databases.

The process of characterizing non-standard residues begins with generating InChI keys, which are standardized, non-proprietary identifiers for chemical substances. The [`generate_inchikeys`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/tools/get_inchi_keys.py#L149) function in [`/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/tools/get_inchi_keys.py`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/tools/get_inchi_keys.py) is responsible for creating these keys for non-standard residues found in a molecular structure. It leverages [`RDKit`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/docs/source/tasks.rst#L20) for chemical structure analysis and handles specific molecular structures like ferroheme. These generated InChI keys serve as a basis for querying external chemical databases.

Ligand identification and characterization are handled by the [`generate_ligand_references`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/tools/get_ligands.py#L90) function in [`/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/tools/get_ligands.py`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/tools/get_ligands.py). This function identifies ligands by matching their InChI keys against the PubChem database, a public repository for chemical information. It supports various matching strategies, including similarity searches based on molecular descriptors. This functionality also integrates data from other specialized chemical databases like DrugBank and ChEMBL to provide a comprehensive characterization of identified ligands.

For lipid identification, the [`generate_lipid_references`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/tools/get_lipids.py#L8) function in [`/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/tools/get_lipids.py`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/tools/get_lipids.py) queries dedicated databases such as SwissLipids and LIPID MAPS. This allows the system to accurately classify and annotate lipid molecules within the simulation, augmenting the chemical information with lipid-specific details. This systematic approach ensures that all non-standard components, including various ligands and lipids, are identified, characterized, and their information is prepared for integration into the workflow.
