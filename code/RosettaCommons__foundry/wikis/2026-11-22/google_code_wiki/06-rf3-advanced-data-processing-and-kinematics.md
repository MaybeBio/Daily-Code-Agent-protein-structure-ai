### RF3 Advanced Data Processing and Kinematics
The RF3 model incorporates advanced data processing pipelines and kinematic calculations to handle diverse input formats and execute complex geometric transformations essential for biomolecular structure prediction.

Data preparation in RF3 is managed by a comprehensive pipeline constructed by [`build_af3_transform_pipeline`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipelines.py#L129) in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipelines.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipelines.py). This pipeline processes raw structural data through various transformations, including [`RemoveHydrogens`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipelines.py#L84) and [`HandleUndesiredResTokens`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipelines.py#L82) for standardization. Key annotations for indexing and identification, such as [`AddGlobalAtomIdAnnotation`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipelines.py#L20) and [`AddGlobalTokenIdAnnotation`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/metrics/lddt.py#L4), are added. Non-polymer residues undergo atomization via [`AtomizeByCCDName`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/metrics/lddt.py#L5), and during training, random atomization can be applied with [`RandomAtomizeResidues`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipelines.py#L98). The pipeline also supports AlphaFold 3-like cropping strategies through [`CropContiguousLikeAF3`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipelines.py#L62) and [`CropSpatialLikeAF3`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipelines.py#L62). Changes introduced by cropping are tracked using [`annotate_pre_crop_hash`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipeline_utils.py#L16) and [`annotate_post_crop_hash`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipeline_utils.py#L22), with [`set_to_occupancy_0_where_crop_hashes_differ`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipeline_utils.py#L28) in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipeline_utils.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipeline_utils.py) masking altered atoms. Multiple Sequence Alignments (MSAs) for proteins and RNA are loaded, paired, merged, and featurized by components like [`LoadPolymerMSAs`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipelines.py#L95) and [`FeaturizeMSALikeAF3`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipelines.py#L93). Token-level features are encoded by [`EncodeAF3TokenLevelFeatures`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/transforms/util_transforms.py#L366), while [`GetAF3ReferenceMoleculeFeatures`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipelines.py#L15) generates reference molecule features, often relying on RDKit for conformer generation. Chiral features are added by [`AddAF3ChiralFeatures`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipelines.py#L58), and bond features, including cyclic bonds detected by [`AddCyclicBonds`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/cyclic_transform.py#L9) in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/cyclic_transform.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/cyclic_transform.py), are computed by [`AddAF3TokenBondFeatures`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipelines.py#L51). Diffusion-specific transforms, such as [`build_ground_truth_distogram_transform`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipeline_utils.py#L45) in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipeline_utils.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipeline_utils.py), prepare noised ground truth distograms for model conditioning. This transform, leveraging [`FeaturizeNoisedGroundTruthAsTemplateDistogram`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/ground_truth_template.py#L387) from [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/ground_truth_template.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/ground_truth_template.py), adds Gaussian noise to atomic coordinates and discretizes pairwise distances into a distogram. The pipeline also handles batching, augmentation, and noise sampling for diffusion models with [`BatchStructuresForDiffusionNoising`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipelines.py#L64) and [`SampleEDMNoise`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/data/pipelines.py#L66).

The model's ability to handle molecular data is supported by definitions of chemical properties and lookup tables in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/chemical.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/chemical.py), which includes [`aa2long`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/chemical.py#L16) for atom names and [`frames`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/chemical.py#L1276) for defining coordinate frames crucial for calculations such as FAPE (Frame Aligned Point Error).

Kinematic calculations are integral to RF3's understanding and manipulation of molecular structures. Functions in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/kinematics.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/kinematics.py) perform essential operations like calculating pairwise distances ([`get_pair_dist`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/kinematics.py#L94)), angles ([`get_ang`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/kinematics.py#L112)), and dihedral angles ([`get_dih`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/kinematics.py#L135)). These utilities also convert 3D Cartesian coordinates to internal coordinate representations (e.g., [`xyz_to_c6d`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/kinematics.py#L181), [`xyz_to_t2d`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/kinematics.py#L231)), which are critical for structural modeling and sampling. Additionally, the system employs utilities for 3D coordinate alignment using [`weighted_rigid_align`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/utils/alignment.py#L8) and RMSD calculations with [`get_rmsd`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/utils/alignment.py#L87) in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/alignment.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/alignment.py). These geometric tools ensure that the model can accurately represent and manipulate biomolecular structures during the prediction process.
