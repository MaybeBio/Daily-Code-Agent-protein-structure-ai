### Pure PyTorch Neighbor List Implementations
The pure PyTorch neighbor list implementations provide reliable fallback mechanisms when highly optimized external libraries, such as those integrated via [`alchemiops_nl_n2`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/neighbors/alchemiops.py#L35) or [`vesin_nl_ts`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/neighbors/vesin.py#L184), are unavailable. These implementations are entirely self-contained within PyTorch, ensuring broad compatibility and avoiding external dependencies. They are particularly relevant for environments where CUDA-enabled hardware or specific [`vesin`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/pyproject.toml#L50) installations are not present.

The [`torch_nl_linked_cell`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/neighbors/torch_nl.py#L156) function, located in [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/neighbors/torch_nl.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/neighbors/torch_nl.py), implements a linked-cell algorithm, offering O(N) complexity for neighbor searching. This approach improves performance scaling for larger systems compared to brute-force methods. For smaller systems or as a simpler alternative, [`torch_nl_n2`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/neighbors/torch_nl.py#L94) in the same file provides an O(N²) naive brute-force neighbor search. Both functions are designed to handle periodic boundary conditions (PBCs) by first wrapping atomic positions into their primary cells. They then utilize [`strict_nl`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/neighbors/torch_nl.py#L25) to filter the initial neighborhood based on a precise cutoff distance. The [`shifts_idx`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/transforms.py#L176) are critical in this process, correctly representing the periodic cell shifts between the original, unwrapped input positions and their neighbors. This careful handling of PBCs ensures that interactions across cell boundaries are accurately captured.

The [`torchsim_nl`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/neighbors/__init__.py#L46) function, defined in [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/neighbors/__init__.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/neighbors/__init__.py), automatically selects one of these pure PyTorch implementations as a last resort, demonstrating their role as robust fallbacks in the overall neighbor list selection strategy. For additional context on the general neighbor list selection process, refer to [Automatic Fallback and Prioritization](#neighbor-list-algorithms-automatic-fallback-and-prioritization).
