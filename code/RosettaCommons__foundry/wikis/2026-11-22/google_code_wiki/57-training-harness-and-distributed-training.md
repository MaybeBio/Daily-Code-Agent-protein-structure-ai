## Training Harness and Distributed Training
Foundry utilizes a generic training harness, implemented as the [`FabricTrainer`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py#L56) class found in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py), built upon PyTorch Lightning Fabric. This harness orchestrates the entire lifecycle of model training, including setup, configuration, validation, and checkpointing, with inherent support for distributed training environments. It integrates features such as Exponential Moving Average (EMA) for model smoothing and improved generalization, and provides support for Intel XPU accelerators, ensuring scalable and efficient model development.

The [`FabricTrainer`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py#L56) manages the initialization and configuration of the training environment, defining parameters such as the accelerator type, distributed strategy, and precision. It maintains a central state that tracks heterogeneous training components like the model, optimizer, scheduler configuration, global step count, and current epoch. This allows for flexible and dynamic construction of models, optimizers, and learning rate schedulers, often leveraging Hydra for configuration, and can wrap models with EMA if specified.

During the training process, the [`FabricTrainer`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py#L56) orchestrates the main training loop, which includes iterating through epochs, managing gradient accumulation, and executing the forward and backward passes. It also handles the logic for stepping the optimizer, applying gradient clipping, and updating the learning rate scheduler based on configurable levels and frequencies. For robust training, it incorporates mechanisms to check for and skip NaN/Inf gradients.

A key aspect of the [`FabricTrainer`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py#L56) is its comprehensive checkpointing system. It allows for saving and loading the complete state of the trainer, including the model, optimizer, and scheduler states. This intelligent restoration capability supports policy-based weight loading and parameter freezing, ensuring continuity and flexibility in training experiments. The system can also identify the latest checkpoint within a specified directory, streamlining the resumption of training.
