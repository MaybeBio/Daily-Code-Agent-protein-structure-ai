### RFD3 Metrics, Utilities, and Testing
RFdiffusion3 (RFD3) employs a range of metrics and utilities to assess *de novo* biomolecular designs, manage data, and support the inference process, ensuring that generated structures align with design principles and can be effectively analyzed and visualized.

A variety of metrics quantify design quality, covering structural integrity, sequence composition, and specific interactions. For instance, the functions and classes within [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/design_metrics.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/design_metrics.py) calculate clash metrics to identify unfavorable atomic overlaps, detect chain breaks, and assess secondary structure elements like helices, strands, and coils. This module also evaluates sequence recovery by comparing predicted sequences against ground truth, and analyzes protein-protein interaction (PPI) hotspots to determine if diffused atoms correctly contact critical regions. The [`AtomArrayMetrics`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/design_metrics.py#L170) class, found in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/design_metrics.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/design_metrics.py), further provides general structural and compositional statistics, including the radius of gyration and amino acid content.

Hydrogen bond quantification is addressed by [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/hbonds_metrics.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/hbonds_metrics.py) and [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/hbonds_hbplus_metrics.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/hbonds_hbplus_metrics.py). These modules process [`AtomArray`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/examples/all.ipynb#L20) objects to identify hydrogen bonds and evaluate the accuracy of predicted donors and acceptors against ground truth, providing metrics on the percentage of correctly predicted H-bonds and their total count. They incorporate preprocessing steps, such as [`simplified_processing_atom_array`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/hbonds_metrics.py#L26), to ensure accurate residue identification and handle virtual atoms, which is crucial for reliable H-bond calculations.

Sidechain quality is assessed by [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/sidechain_metrics.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/sidechain_metrics.py). This includes functions like [`collapsing_virtual_atoms_batched`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/sidechain_metrics.py#L9) to identify and remove virtual atoms that indicate collapsed sidechains, and [`check_sidechain_quality`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/sidechain_metrics.py#L114) which compares the topology of generated sidechains to standard amino acid structures using graph-based methods. It also identifies unintended bonds between sidechains of different residues and intra-residue clashes.

Loss functions are defined in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/losses.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/losses.py), which includes [`SequenceLoss`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/metrics/losses.py#L7) for token-level cross-entropy on sequence predictions and [`DiffusionLoss`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/loss/af3_losses.py#L416) for coordinate-based mean squared error. The [`DiffusionLoss`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/loss/af3_losses.py#L416) can incorporate an optional [`smoothed_lddt_loss`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/loss/af3_losses.py#L544), which measures structural similarity by comparing inter-atomic distances. These loss functions are critical for guiding model training.

Beyond metrics, the RFD3 framework includes utilities for [`AtomArray`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/examples/all.ipynb#L20) preparation, I/O operations, and visualization. The [`inference`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/README.md#L120) utility in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/utils/inference.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/utils/inference.py) assists with [`AtomArray`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/examples/all.ipynb#L20) preparation and structural patching, while the [`io`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/.env#L57) utility in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/utils/io.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/utils/io.py) handles saving generated structures, metadata, and trajectories. For visualizing results, the [`vizualize`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/pyproject.toml#L284) utility in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/utils/vizualize.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/src/rfd3/utils/vizualize.py) provides functions for PyMOL. The comprehensive [`pytest`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/pyproject.toml#L295) suite, located in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/tests`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rfd3/tests), ensures the correctness and consistency of these components, covering core functionality, bond preservation, conditioning logic, pipeline equivalence, and symmetry properties.
