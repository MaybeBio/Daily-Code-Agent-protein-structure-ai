### Training Harness with PyTorch Lightning Fabric
Foundry's generic training harness, [`FabricTrainer`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py#L56), is built upon PyTorch Lightning Fabric and orchestrates the entire lifecycle of model training and validation. It is defined in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py). The [`FabricTrainer`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py#L56) manages the setup of accelerators and strategies, configuring how training utilizes hardware resources and distributed computing. This includes handling [`XPUAccelerator`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/utils/xpu/xpu_accelerator.py#L9) and [`SingleXPUStrategy`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/utils/xpu/single_xpu_strategy.py#L13) for Intel XPU devices.

The trainer initializes and updates its internal state, which includes the model, optimizer, scheduler configuration, and global training parameters. It dynamically constructs essential components such as the model, optimizers, and learning rate schedulers, often leveraging Hydra for flexible configuration. Support for Exponential Moving Average (EMA) is integrated, allowing models to be wrapped with EMA during construction for improved generalization.

The core of the [`FabricTrainer`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py#L56) functionality lies in its training and validation loops. The [`fit`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py#L329) method orchestrates the overall training process, iterating through epochs and managing checkpoint loading and validation. The [`train_loop`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py#L510) executes a single training epoch, managing gradient accumulation, stepping the optimizer, and updating the scheduler. Similarly, the [`validation_loop`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py#L598) runs a single validation epoch, switching the model to evaluation mode, collecting metrics, and then restoring the training mode. Subclasses of [`FabricTrainer`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py#L56) implement abstract [`training_step`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py#L661) and [`validation_step`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py#L681) methods to define the specific forward and backward passes for each batch.

The [`FabricTrainer`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py#L56) also handles optimizer steps, including mechanisms to skip updates if NaN or Inf gradients are detected and applying gradient clipping to prevent exploding gradients. It ensures that EMA parameters are updated when enabled. Robust checkpointing mechanisms are in place for saving and loading the complete trainer state, including model weights, optimizer states, and scheduler configurations. This allows for intelligent restoration of training progress and flexible loading policies, such as freezing specific parameters, as described in [Model Weight Management and Parameter Freezing](#general-utilities-for-deep-learning-infrastructure-model-weight-management-and-parameter-freezing). The [`FabricTrainer`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/src/foundry/trainers/fabric.py#L56) also includes utility functions for checking the environment (e.g., interactive mode) and providing rank-zero-only logging to avoid redundant output in distributed settings.
