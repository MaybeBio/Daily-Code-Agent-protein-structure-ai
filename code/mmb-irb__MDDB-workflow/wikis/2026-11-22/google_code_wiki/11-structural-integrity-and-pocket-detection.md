### Structural Integrity and Pocket Detection
Tools for verifying the structural integrity of molecular dynamics trajectories, and methods for detecting and characterizing protein pockets, are provided within the workflow.

The structural integrity of trajectories is checked by detecting sudden Root Mean Square Deviation (RMSD) jumps between consecutive frames, which can indicate simulation artifacts or instability. This process, implemented in [`check_trajectory_integrity`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/analyses/rmsd_check.py#L29) within [`/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/analyses/rmsd_check.py`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/analyses/rmsd_check.py), calculates RMSD for entire molecular fragments or on a per-residue basis to identify outliers. The detection relies on z-scores of RMSD jumps; values exceeding a [`standard_deviations_cutoff`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/mwf.py#L1222) are flagged. This analysis considers user-defined selections for atomic groups and applies a fragment-based approach to localize issues, preventing small localized jumps from being masked by overall stability. A more granular, per-residue RMSD jump analysis is also available, leveraging [`pytraj`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/envs/environment.yml#L10) for calculations.

Protein pocket detection and characterization are handled by the [`pockets`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/analyses/pockets.py#L45) function in [`/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/analyses/pockets.py`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/analyses/pockets.py). This analysis utilizes [`MDpocket`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/analyses/pockets.py#L6) to identify and characterize druggable pockets from molecular dynamics trajectories. The process involves reducing the trajectory to a manageable number of frames, running [`MDpocket`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/analyses/pockets.py#L6) to generate a frequency grid of pocket occurrences, and then identifying and clustering these regions based on connectivity. For each significant pocket, [`MDpocket`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/analyses/pockets.py#L6) is re-run to extract dynamic data such as pocket volumes and implicated atoms. The results are then stored in a JSON file for further analysis.
