### Results Management and Persistent Storage
Experimental results, including predicted PDBs and various evaluation metrics, are organized and persistently stored within the [`experiments`](https://github.com/aqlaboratory/openfold/openfold/config.py#L608) directory. The evaluation script, found at [`/aqlaboratory/openfold/experiments/test_templates_openfold.py`](https://github.com/aqlaboratory/openfold/experiments/test_templates_openfold.py), manages the saving of predicted protein structures as PDB files and appends all collected evaluation metrics to CSV files. To ensure reproducibility and efficient processing, the system tracks [`finished_decoys`](https://github.com/aqlaboratory/openfold/experiments/test_templates_openfold.py#L382) and [`finished_targets`](https://github.com/aqlaboratory/openfold/experiments/test_templates_openfold.py#L390), enabling the resumption of interrupted runs without redundant computations. This persistent storage mechanism allows for comprehensive analysis of model performance and comparison across different experiments. For a deeper understanding of the evaluation process, refer to [Workflow for Decoy Scoring and Evaluation](#experimentation-and-model-evaluation-workflow-for-decoy-scoring-and-evaluation).
