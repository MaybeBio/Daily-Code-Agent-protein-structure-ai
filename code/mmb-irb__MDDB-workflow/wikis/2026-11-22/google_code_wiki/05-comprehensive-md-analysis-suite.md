### Comprehensive MD Analysis Suite
The [`mddb-workflow`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/README.md#L4) repository provides a suite of tools for in-depth analysis of molecular dynamics (MD) simulation data, encompassing a wide range of biophysical and structural calculations. This analytical framework is primarily contained within the [`/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/analyses`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/analyses) directory. It supports the characterization of membrane properties, conformational changes, and intermolecular interactions.

For analyzing membrane systems, the workflow can compute the Area Per Lipid (APL) using external tools to determine the surface area occupied by each lipid molecule. This process also grids the APL data and generates contour plots to visualize the distribution across the membrane. Channel analysis, particularly for protein pores, is performed using specialized external software to identify and characterize channels, including parsing pore-lining and pore-facing residues from its output. Lipid order parameters (S) are also calculated for all-atom, united-atom, and coarse-grained simulations, providing insights into membrane fluidity and lipid acyl chain orientation. Further details can be found in [Specialized Biophysical Analyses](#molecular-dynamics-analysis-tools-specialized-biophysical-analyses).

Conformational dynamics are explored through various methods. Clustering algorithms group similar conformations together based on RMSD, identifying representative frames and analyzing transitions between clusters. Principal Component Analysis (PCA) is applied to trajectories to identify the dominant modes of motion, and can also be used to analyze changes in inter-residue contacts. Additionally, tools are available to generate data suitable for Markov State Models (MSM), which describe the kinetics and thermodynamics of conformational transitions. See [Conformational Sampling and Dynamics Analyses](#molecular-dynamics-analysis-tools-conformational-sampling-and-dynamics-analyses) for more information.

Interaction analyses quantify the energetic and structural relationships between different molecular components. This includes the calculation of dihedral energies (torsion, electrostatic, and Van der Waals) for specific rotatable bonds, providing insights into local flexibility and stability. Inter-residue distances are analyzed to map interaction networks, and hydrogen bond analysis identifies and quantifies these critical interactions. Furthermore, interaction energies between agents (e.g., protein-ligand, protein-protein) are computed using specialized tools that handle electrostatic and Van der Waals contributions. Lipid-protein interactions are specifically analyzed to classify contacts by lipid type and their head/tail parts. More detailed information can be found in [Intermolecular and Intramolecular Interaction Analyses](#molecular-dynamics-analysis-tools-intermolecular-and-intramolecular-interaction-analyses).

The suite also includes analyses for structural integrity and pocket detection. RMSD checks help verify the stability of trajectories by identifying sudden jumps that might indicate simulation artifacts. Tools for detecting and characterizing protein pockets, including their volume and implicated atoms, contribute to understanding potential binding sites. Consult [Structural Integrity and Pocket Detection](#molecular-dynamics-analysis-tools-structural-integrity-and-pocket-detection) for further reading.

Overall, the analytical tools leverage several external libraries such as [`mdtraj`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/envs/environment.yml#L22), [`pytraj`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/envs/environment.yml#L10), and [`MDAnalysis`](https://github.com/mmb-irb/mddb-workflow/mddb_workflow/mwf.py#L877) for core molecular manipulation, alongside specialized third-party applications for specific calculations. The results are consistently saved in JSON format for easy integration with downstream analysis and visualization workflows.
