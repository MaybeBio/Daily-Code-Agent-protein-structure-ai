### NVT Integrator Implementations
The [`torch-sim`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/README.md#L3) framework offers several implementations for NVT (canonical ensemble) integrators, enabling molecular dynamics simulations at constant temperature. These integrators are designed for batched operations and leverage PyTorch for accelerated computations. The available NVT methods include Langevin dynamics, Nose-Hoover Chains (NHC), and Velocity Rescaling (CSVR), each managing the simulation state and advancing the system through distinct integration steps. The choice of integrator impacts how temperature is controlled and how the system samples the canonical ensemble.

Langevin dynamics is implemented using the BAOAB splitting scheme. This method applies both frictional forces and random noise to the particles' momenta, drawing from an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process to ensure proper thermalization. The core steps involve updating momenta, positions, and then applying the stochastic and dissipative forces. The initialization and stepping functions for this method are found in [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py), specifically [`nvt_langevin_init`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py#L89) and [`nvt_langevin_step`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py#L145). The underlying [`MDState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/md.py#L32) (see [Core MD State and Operations](#simulation-integrators-core-md-state-and-operations)) is extended to manage these operations.

For a more deterministic approach to temperature control, Nose-Hoover Chains (NHC) are provided. This method introduces additional fictitious particles (thermostats) that couple to the system's kinetic energy, maintaining the desired temperature without stochastic noise. The NHC implementation in [`torch-sim`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/README.md#L3) follows Martyna et al.'s direct translation method, employing Suzuki-Yoshida splitting for robust integration of the chain variables. The [`NVTNoseHooverState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py#L244) in [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py) extends [`MDState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/md.py#L32) to incorporate the chain's state variables and helper functions. The [`nvt_nose_hoover_init`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py#L293) function initializes the system and the Nose-Hoover chain, while [`nvt_nose_hoover_step`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py#L381) orchestrates the evolution of both the physical system and the thermostat chain. The conserved quantity for the NHC ensemble can be calculated using [`nvt_nose_hoover_invariant`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py#L491) to verify the simulation's correctness.

Finally, Velocity Rescaling (CSVR) offers another method for thermostatting, based on the algorithm proposed by Bussi, Donadio, and Parrinello. This thermostat periodically rescales particle velocities to match the target temperature, incorporating a stochastic component to ensure canonical sampling. The [`NVTVRescaleState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py#L565) in [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py) is specialized for this method. The [`_vrescale_update`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py#L594) function within [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py) handles the actual velocity rescaling logic, which is then integrated into the [`nvt_vrescale_step`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py#L715) function to advance the simulation.

All NVT integrators leverage a [`ModelInterface`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/models/interface.py#L71) to abstract away force and energy calculations, ensuring flexibility with various potential models. The system's state is encapsulated in [`MDState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/md.py#L32) or its specialized extensions, facilitating clear management of physical properties and integration variables. The overall selection and setup of these integrators are handled by the [Integrator Registry and Unit Conversion System](#simulation-integrators-integrator-registry-and-unit-conversion-system), which ensures proper unit handling and dynamic selection of integration schemes.
