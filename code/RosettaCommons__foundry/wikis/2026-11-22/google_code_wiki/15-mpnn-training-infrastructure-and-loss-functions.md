### MPNN Training Infrastructure and Loss Functions
The training infrastructure for MPNN models centers around efficient data handling, distributed learning, and a specialized loss function. The training process, orchestrated by the [`MPNNTrainer`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/trainers/mpnn.py#L21), utilizes token-budgeted batching and sophisticated data collators to optimize GPU memory usage and computational efficiency.

Data handling begins with [`PaddedTokenBudgetBatchSampler`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/samplers/samplers.py#L9) (defined in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/samplers/samplers.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/samplers/samplers.py) and further detailed in [MPNN Data Preparation and Transformation Pipelines](#protein-and-ligand-inverse-folding-mpnn-mpnn-data-preparation-and-transformation-pipelines)). This sampler ensures that batches are constructed with a token budget in mind, minimizing padding by grouping samples of similar lengths. This is achieved by sorting samples by their token counts and then greedily forming batches that adhere to a [`max_tokens_with_padding`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/train.py#L211) limit. The sampler also provides an epoch-based caching mechanism to optimize batch generation. Complementing this, [`TokenBudgetAwareFeatureCollator`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/collate/feature_collator.py#L187) (found in [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/collate/feature_collator.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/collate/feature_collator.py)) handles the actual batch collation, managing variable-length features through padding and ensuring consistency of scalar features across the batch. It filters examples to ensure that the token budget is met, dropping larger examples if necessary.

The core training logic resides within the [`MPNNTrainer`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/trainers/mpnn.py#L21) class (located at [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/trainers/mpnn.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/trainers/mpnn.py) and elaborated in [MPNN Model Architecture and Core Functionality](#protein-and-ligand-inverse-folding-mpnn-mpnn-model-architecture-and-core-functionality)). This trainer, built upon PyTorch Lightning Fabric, manages the entire training and validation lifecycle. It constructs either [`ProteinMPNN`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/model/mpnn.py#L21) or [`LigandMPNN`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/model/mpnn.py#L2303) models and employs a [`LabelSmoothedNLLLoss`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/loss/nll_loss.py#L7) (see [`/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/loss/nll_loss.py`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/loss/nll_loss.py)) as its fixed loss function. This loss function incorporates label smoothing to prevent overconfidence in predictions and normalizes the aggregated loss by a constant value. The [`MPNNTrainer`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/mpnn/src/mpnn/trainers/mpnn.py#L21) also handles distributed training effectively by leveraging [`fabric.no_backward_sync`](https://github.com/rosettacommons/foundry/models/rf3/src/rf3/trainers/rf3.py#L230) for gradient accumulation during distributed data parallel (DDP) setups, enhancing scalability. The training flow includes a Noam-style learning rate scheduler to dynamically adjust the learning rate during different training phases, and robust checkpointing mechanisms for saving and restoring model states (for more details on general training infrastructure, see [Training Harness with PyTorch Lightning Fabric](#foundry-core-library-and-ml-workflows-training-harness-with-pytorch-lightning-fabric)).
