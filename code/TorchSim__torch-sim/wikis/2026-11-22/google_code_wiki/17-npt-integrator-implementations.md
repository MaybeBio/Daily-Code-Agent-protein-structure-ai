### NPT Integrator Implementations
The [`torch-sim`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/README.md#L3) framework includes various NPT (isothermal-isobaric ensemble) integrators within [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py) to simulate systems at constant particle number, pressure, and temperature. These integrators extend the core [`MDState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/md.py#L32) with pressure-related attributes and manage cell dynamics in addition to particle motion. The NPT integrators primarily differ in their barostat implementations and how they couple pressure control with temperature control. The core [`NPTState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py#L41) extends the general [`MDState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/md.py#L32) by incorporating the [`stress`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/runners.py#L905) tensor, which is crucial for pressure calculations.

**Langevin Barostats**

Two Langevin-based NPT integrators are available, both drawing inspiration from the Gronbech-Jensen & Farago (2014) method and LAMMPS's [`fix press/langevin`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py#L67). These methods introduce additional degrees of freedom to control the system's cell dimensions, similar to how Langevin dynamics controls particle velocities.

*   **Anisotropic Langevin Barostat**: This implementation, utilizing [`NPTLangevinAnisotropicState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py#L62), manages the simulation cell's shape and volume anisotropically. It introduces logarithmic strain coordinates ([`cell_positions`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py#L108)) for each dimension, along with their corresponding velocities and masses. This allows for independent fluctuations and control over the three lattice vectors. The integration step ([`npt_langevin_anisotropic_step`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py#L513)) involves a four-step splitting scheme that updates particle positions and velocities, as well as the cell dimensions, while applying Langevin noise and friction to both particles and the cell coordinates. The force on the cell's strain coordinates is computed from the system's stress, kinetic energy, volume, and external pressure.

*   **Isotropic Langevin Barostat**: The [`NPTLangevinIsotropicState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py#L653) provides a simplified approach where only the system's volume is controlled, assuming an isotropic scaling of the cell. A single isotropic logarithmic strain coordinate ([`ε`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/elastic.py#L154)) dictates the volume change. The [`npt_langevin_isotropic_step`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py#L988) integrates the system by isotropically scaling the cell based on this single strain variable, applying similar Langevin dynamics principles to manage the volume fluctuations.

**Nose-Hoover Barostats**

The Nose-Hoover barostat for NPT simulations is implemented using [`NPTNoseHooverIsotropicState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py#L1110). This method employs Nose-Hoover chains, not only for temperature control but also for pressure control. It extends the concept of a thermostat to a barostat by coupling an additional Nose-Hoover chain to the cell's volume degree of freedom. This implementation typically uses a logarithmic cell coordinate for volume, similar to the isotropic Langevin method. The [`npt_nose_hoover_isotropic_init`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py#L1642) function configures two [`NoseHooverChain`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/md.py#L249) instances: one for the thermostat and another for the barostat. The [`npt_nose_hoover_isotropic_step`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py#L1812) uses a symmetric Trotter factorization to integrate the system, handling both particle and cell dynamics, while ensuring the conservation of an extended Hamiltonian. This conserved quantity can be monitored for verifying simulation correctness. For more details on the underlying Nose-Hoover chain mechanics, refer to [Core MD State and Operations](#simulation-integrators-core-md-state-and-operations).

**C-Rescale Barostats**

The C-Rescale barostat, represented by [`NPTCRescaleState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py#L2062), offers a stochastic cell rescaling approach for NPT simulations. This method is based on Del Tatto et al. (2022) and is particularly useful for controlling cell dynamics under various conditions.

*   **Triclinic C-Rescale Barostat**: The [`_crescale_triclinic_barostat_step`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py#L2186) function implements an anisotropic stochastic cell rescaling step for general triclinic cells. It manages volume and deformation updates through a three-step process. The [`npt_crescale_triclinic_step`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py#L2517) integrates the system using a Trotter splitting scheme that combines velocity rescaling for temperature control with the triclinic barostat step.

The NPT integrators leverage [`torch.Tensor`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/CHANGELOG.md#L90) for all numerical computations, enabling efficient GPU acceleration for batched simulations. Each integrator maintains its specific state using [`dataclass`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/state.py#L13) structures, promoting clear and maintainable code. The overall system for managing integrators and their parameters is described in [Integrator Registry and Unit Conversion System](#simulation-integrators-integrator-registry-and-unit-conversion-system).
