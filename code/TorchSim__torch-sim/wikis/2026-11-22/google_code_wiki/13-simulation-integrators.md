## Simulation Integrators
Molecular dynamics (MD) integrators facilitate the time evolution of atomistic systems under specific thermodynamic conditions. [`torch-sim`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/README.md#L3) provides a suite of PyTorch-accelerated MD integrators for batched simulations with periodic boundary conditions, supporting various ensembles, including NVE (microcanonical), NVT (canonical, constant temperature), and NPT (isothermal-isobaric, constant temperature and pressure).

The core of MD simulations revolves around updating particle positions and momenta. The [`MDState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/md.py#L32) dataclass in [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/md.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/md.py) serves as the central container for these simulation variables, alongside attributes like energy, forces, masses, cell, and periodic boundary conditions. Fundamental operations such as [`initialize_momenta`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/md.py#L113) for setting initial velocities, [`momentum_step`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/md.py#L169) for updating momenta, and [`position_step`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/md.py#L190) for advancing particle positions are also defined here. Many integrators build upon the [`velocity_verlet_step`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/md.py#L211), which implements the Velocity Verlet algorithm—a time-reversible and symplectic method for conserving total energy in MD.

The framework manages a wide array of integrators through the [`INTEGRATOR_REGISTRY`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/__init__.py#L193) in [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/__init__.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/__init__.py). This registry maps each [`Integrator`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/__init__.py#L125) enum value to its corresponding initialization and stepping functions, enabling dynamic selection and execution of different integration schemes. This design ensures that the specific logic for each ensemble is modularly organized.

Unit conversion is automatically handled for integrator parameters, ensuring consistency and ease of use. The system inspects type hints in integrator function signatures to collect unit metadata, allowing for automatic conversion of user-provided values to internal units. This mechanism, facilitated by [`_collect_unit_kwargs`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/__init__.py#L231) and [`INTEGRATOR_KWARG_UNITS`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/__init__.py#L251) in [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/__init__.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/__init__.py), simplifies the interface for users by abstracting away unit management.

For constant temperature simulations, [`torch-sim`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/README.md#L3) offers several NVT integrators, detailed in [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py). These include Langevin dynamics, which uses a BAOAB splitting scheme to apply stochastic noise and friction for thermal sampling. Alternatively, the Nose-Hoover chain thermostat, with its [`NVTNoseHooverState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nvt.py#L244), provides a deterministic approach to temperature control. The Velocity Rescaling (CSVR) thermostat, another option, re-scales particle velocities to maintain a target temperature.

For simulations at constant temperature and pressure, the framework provides NPT integrators in [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py). These often extend the [`NPTState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py#L41) to include the system's stress tensor. Implementations include Langevin barostats, which can be configured for anisotropic or isotropic strain control, and Nose-Hoover barostats, which use chains for both temperature and pressure regulation. Additionally, stochastic cell rescaling (C-Rescale) barostats are available for triclinic, anisotropic, and isotropic conditions, offering different approaches to controlling the system's volume and shape. All these integrators leverage [`torch.Tensor`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/CHANGELOG.md#L90) operations for GPU acceleration, optimizing performance for large-scale simulations.
