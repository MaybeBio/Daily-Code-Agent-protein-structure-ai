### Subcell Extraction and Structural Validation
Subcells can be extracted from larger atomic structures, such as those generated from a random packed configuration. The [`get_subcells_to_crystallize`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/workflows/a2c.py#L504) function in [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/workflows/a2c.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/workflows/a2c.py) systematically identifies these subcells using a grid-based approach. This function allows for constraints on compositional purity and atom count, ensuring that extracted subcells are chemically relevant. Once identified, the [`subcells_to_structures`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/workflows/a2c.py#L616) function, also found in [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/workflows/a2c.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/workflows/a2c.py), converts these subcell indices and their boundaries into distinct structure tuples.

After extraction, these structures undergo validation to ensure their physical plausibility and the success of any prior optimization steps. The [`valid_subcell`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/workflows/a2c.py#L434) function in [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/workflows/a2c.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/workflows/a2c.py) performs this validation by checking criteria such as the formation energy, the energy reduction achieved during relaxation, and the absence of atomic fusion (i.e., atoms being too close to each other). The [`min_distance`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/workflows/a2c.py#L26) function in [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/workflows/a2c.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/workflows/a2c.py) is used to confirm that no atoms are unphysically close, which is a critical aspect of structural integrity. These validation steps help filter out unphysical or poorly optimized structures, retaining only those suitable for further analysis or downstream applications within workflows such as the [Crystallization Workflow with A2C Method](#advanced-workflows-and-research-applications-crystallization-workflow-with-a2c-method).
