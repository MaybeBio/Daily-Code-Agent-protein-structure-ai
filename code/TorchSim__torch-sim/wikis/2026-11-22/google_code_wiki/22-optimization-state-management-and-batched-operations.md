### Optimization State Management and Batched Operations
The [`torch-sim`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/README.md#L3) optimization framework employs a hierarchical state management system, designed to efficiently handle batched atomistic simulations with varying system sizes and diverse constraints. The foundation of this system is the [`SimState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/state.py#L121) dataclass, which encapsulates general atomistic system properties.

Optimization-specific states extend [`SimState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/state.py#L121) to include parameters relevant to the minimization process. The base [`OptimState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py#L12), defined in [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py), adds attributes like [`forces`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/runners.py#L904), [`energy`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/units.py#L104), and [`stress`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/runners.py#L905), which are fundamental for any optimization algorithm. A key aspect of [`OptimState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py#L12) is its [`set_constrained_forces`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py#L27) method, which ensures that any calculated forces adhere to predefined constraints before being applied, maintaining physical or user-defined boundaries on atomic movement.

Specialized optimization algorithms further extend [`OptimState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py#L12) to manage their unique requirements:
*   [`FireState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py#L39), used by the [NVE Integrator Implementation](#simulation-integrators-nve-integrator-implementation), incorporates attributes such as [`velocities`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/md.py#L74), [`dt`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py#L46) (timestep), [`alpha`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py#L102), and [`n_pos`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py#L48) to manage the adaptive stepping and momentum updates characteristic of the FIRE algorithm.
*   [`BFGSState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py#L55) maintains the approximate Hessian matrix ([`hessian`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py#L76)), along with [`prev_forces`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py#L77) and [`prev_positions`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py#L78), which are crucial for the BFGS algorithm's iterative updates.
*   [`LBFGSState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py#L102) is designed for the limited-memory BFGS algorithm, storing [`s_history`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py#L129) (displacement history) and [`y_history`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py#L130) (gradient-difference history) for its two-loop recursion.

For optimizations involving unit cell parameters, the framework introduces specialized [`CellOptimState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/cell_filters.py#L427) dataclasses (e.g., [`CellFireState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/cell_filters.py#L458), [`CellBFGSState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/cell_filters.py#L471), [`CellLBFGSState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/cell_filters.py#L499)). These extend their respective base optimizer states with cell-specific attributes, including a [`reference_cell`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/state.py#L866), [`cell_filter`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/cell_filters.py#L431) configuration, [`cell_positions`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/npt.py#L108), and [`cell_forces`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/cell_filters.py#L438). This enables the simultaneous relaxation of both atomic positions and cell dimensions, while integrating with the filter-based design for cell optimization constraints described in [Cell Optimization and Filter-Based Design](#geometry-optimization-algorithms-cell-optimization-and-filter-based-design).

A critical design consideration for these state objects is their ability to handle batches of systems with varying numbers of atoms. This is achieved through mechanisms like [`_padded_system_attributes`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py#L98), which identifies attributes requiring padding to [`global_max_atoms`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/bfgs.py#L124) for efficient batched tensor operations, as detailed in the implementations for [Optimizer Implementations](#geometry-optimization-algorithms-optimizer-implementations) and [Optimizer Implementations](#geometry-optimization-algorithms-optimizer-implementations). This approach allows the framework to perform operations on heterogeneous batches, simplifying tensor computations while maintaining flexibility for different system sizes. The [`atom_idx_in_system`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/optimizers/state.py#L82) attribute further facilitates the mapping between global padded tensors and individual system data, ensuring accurate per-system updates.
