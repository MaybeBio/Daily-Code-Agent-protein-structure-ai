### NVE Integrator Implementation
The NVE (microcanonical ensemble) integrator is designed to simulate atomic systems while conserving the total energy. It is implemented within [`/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nve.py`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nve.py) and relies on the core molecular dynamics state management defined in [Core MD State and Operations](#simulation-integrators-core-md-state-and-operations).

The simulation process begins with an initialization function, [`nve_init`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nve.py#L19), which prepares an [`MDState`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/md.py#L32) for NVE integration. This involves calculating initial potential energies and forces using the designated potential model. If initial momenta (velocities) are not explicitly provided, [`nve_init`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nve.py#L19) samples them from a Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution corresponding to a specified temperature. This ensures the system starts with appropriate kinetic energy while removing any net center-of-mass motion to prevent unintended system drift.

Following initialization, the system's time evolution is managed by the [`nve_step`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nve.py#L74) function, which performs a single integration step. This function employs the Velocity Verlet algorithm, a numerical integration scheme known for its accuracy, time-reversibility, and conservation properties crucial for microcanonical simulations. The Velocity Verlet algorithm updates particle positions and momenta in a symmetric fashion: a half-step update of momenta, a full-step update of positions, recalculation of forces and potential energy at the new positions, and finally, another half-step update of momenta. This sequence ensures that the total energy of the system remains conserved over long simulation times. Both [`nve_init`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nve.py#L19) and [`nve_step`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/integrators/nve.py#L74) interact with a [`ModelInterface`](https://github.com/torchsim/torch-sim/torch_sim/models/interface.py#L71) to abstract the details of force and energy calculations, allowing for flexibility in the choice of interatomic potentials.
